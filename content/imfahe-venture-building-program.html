Transforming Ideas into Ventures. Connecting Talent with Global Innovation Ecosystems.

International Entrepreneurship Initiatives

  1. Since 2021

    ChampU & Richi Entrepreneurs | Boston, USA

    International Entrepreneurship Experience

    In collaboration with the University of Oviedo and Richi Foundation, with funding from Banco Sabadell, IMFAHE supports the selection of the TalentU Hackathon winner for a 10-day entrepreneurship experience in Boston through the Richi LifeSciences Entrepreneurs Program.

    Participants gain exposure to Boston’s innovation ecosystem, international entrepreneurship, and startup development.

  2. July 2024

    Fomento San Sebastián – EKINN Campus | San Sebastián, Spain

    Boston Innovation Ecosystem & Startup Development

    IMFAHE delivered a practical entrepreneurship workshop exploring how to launch a startup, develop an effective investor pitch, and learn from successful founders.

    The experience included insights into Boston’s entrepreneurial ecosystem and an interactive panel featuring founders sharing their experiences.

  3. 2023

    ARKUOS Global Challenge for Future Entrepreneurs | Asturias, Spain

    Innovation, Sustainability & Startup Creation

    Inspired by IMFAHE’s entrepreneurship competition model, this two-day in person program challenged young entrepreneurs to develop business solutions addressing sustainability challenges in agri-food systems, biotechnology, artificial intelligence, and renewable energy.

    Participants received international mentorship, entrepreneurship training, and pitching guidance before presenting their projects to an expert jury.

  4. November 2023

    Fomento San Sebastián – Emprende Conference | San Sebastián, Spain

    Building a Global Entrepreneurial Mindset

    IMFAHE delivered an online lecture on Building an Entrepreneurial Global Mindset Towards Success, exploring how international networking, innovation, and global thinking contribute to entrepreneurial success.
