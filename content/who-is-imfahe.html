Who We Are

IMFAHE is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization founded in 2015 and headquartered in Massachusetts, USA.

We bridge the gap between academia and the global professional world through Ivy League-inspired education, international mentoring programs, and a global talent and entrepreneurship platform connecting students, researchers, professionals, universities, and innovation ecosystems with world-class mentors, professional development opportunities, and international networks.

By building a global infrastructure for talent development, scientific collaboration, innovation, and entrepreneurship, IMFAHE empowers universities, research institutions, and innovation ecosystems to accelerate career development, foster startup creation and growth, expand international research collaborations, enhance global visibility, and drive cross-border partnerships.
