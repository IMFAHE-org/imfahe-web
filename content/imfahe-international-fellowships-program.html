International Excellence Fellowships

How to Apply for IMFAHE International Fellowships

Apply to join IMFAHE’s Global Talent Platform through its open calls. Selected members receive international mentoring for one academic year and are eligible to apply for that year’s fellowships and funded internships abroad.
