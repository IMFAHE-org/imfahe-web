How Does the International Mentoring Program Work?

A structured, personalized mentoring experience throughout the academic year.

  1. Join the Global Talent Network

    Apply through your IMFAHE partner university’s annual selection process.

  2. Get Matched with an International Mentor

    Connect with a professional whose expertise aligns with your academic background and career aspirations.

  3. Define Your Career Goals

    Set personalized development objectives with your mentor and identify practical steps to achieve them.

  4. Grow Through International Mentorship

    Build skills and gain global perspectives through regular online meetings and ongoing guidance.
