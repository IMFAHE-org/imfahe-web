Privacy Notice

What this website collects about you, and why.

Draft for review by IMFAHE.

Who we are

This website belongs to the International Mentoring Foundation for the Advancement of Higher Education (IMFAHE), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization founded in 2015 and headquartered in Massachusetts, USA. For any question about your data, write to contact@imfahe.org.

What we collect when you read this website

You can read every page without an account and without giving us any information. The website sets no cookies of its own.

  • Hosting. The website is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical data when your browser asks for a page, such as your IP address, and may keep it in its logs for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Your cookie choice. Your answer to the cookie question is saved in your own browser, so we do not ask again on every page. It is not sent to us.
  • Statistics, only if you accept. If you choose “Accept”, Google Analytics records which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are, and what kind of device and browser they use. It sets cookies to do this. If you choose “Decline”, it never loads. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Videos. Videos are hosted on YouTube. Nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play; from then on, YouTube receives data about your visit under Google’s privacy policy.

What we collect when you contact us or apply

Some links open an email to IMFAHE or a Google Form, for example to register as a mentor. We use what you send us only to answer you and to manage your participation in our programs. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

Calls to join the Global Platform of Talent are run by each partner university, on its own website and under its own privacy rules. IMFAHE Connects is a separate platform, with its own login and its own terms.

Where your data is processed

IMFAHE is based in the United States, and so are the services named above (GitHub and Google). If you visit from another country, your data may be processed in the United States.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to contact@imfahe.org. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when the website changes how it handles data. Last updated: November 2026.
