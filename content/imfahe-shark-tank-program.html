What Is the IMFAHE Global Entrepreneurship Competition?

The IMFAHE Global Entrepreneurship Competition is an annual international startup and innovation competition organized by IMFAHE Foundation in collaboration with partner universities and experienced mentors worldwide.

Participants collaborate across disciplines and countries to develop innovative business ideas, explore market opportunities, and create solutions with commercial or societal impact.

The competition welcomes innovation across multiple sectors, including science and technology, healthcare and biotechnology, education, sustainability, social impact, and emerging business models.

Our mission is to empower the next generation of global entrepreneurs by connecting talent, expertise, and opportunities.

How Does the Global Entrepreneurship Competition Work?

IMFAHE launches its Global Entrepreneurship Competition call in January. Only selected students, professors, researchers, and mentors in IMFAHE’s Global Platform of Talent are eligible to participate.

  1. Join IMFAHE’s Global Talent Platform

    Become an eligible member through IMFAHE’s annual selection process.

  2. Build Your International Team

    Connect with innovators worldwide through IMFAHE Connects, exchange ideas, and form multidisciplinary teams.

  3. Develop Your Startup Idea

    Between January and March, access IMFAHE Global Academy training with entrepreneurs, technology transfer experts, and venture capital professionals.

    Develop your business model, validate your idea, and prepare your project.

  4. Enter the Competition

    Submit your innovative business proposal according to the annual competition guidelines and deadlines.

  5. Pitch & Compete for Funding

    Selected teams pitch to an international expert panel through IMFAHE Shark Tank, competing for recognition, entrepreneurship awards, and available pre-seed funding.

  6. Advance Your Entrepreneurial Journey

    Use the knowledge, feedback, and international connections gained to further develop your venture.

Eligibility, dates, selection criteria, and funding are subject to the annual call.

Who Can Participate?

The competition is open to eligible members of IMFAHE’s Global Platform of Talent, including university students, PhD candidates, postdoctoral researchers, scientists, academics, and early-career professionals interested in entrepreneurship.

You don’t need an established startup to get started. An innovative idea, an entrepreneurial mindset, and the motivation to make an impact are the first steps.

Specific eligibility requirements, application deadlines, and funding conditions are announced in each annual competition call.
