International Excellence Fellowships

IMFAHE Fellowship Winners

IMFAHE offers funded internships, research fellowships and hands-on training at leading universities, research centers and other organizations worldwide.

Work with an international mentor to strengthen your CV and prepare for competitive applications.

Fellows gain hands-on research experience, develop their skills and work with international teams. IMFAHE funds these opportunities with academic institutions, research organizations and donors.

How to Apply for IMFAHE International Fellowships

Apply to join IMFAHE’s Global Talent Platform through its open calls. Selected members receive international mentoring for one academic year and are eligible to apply for that year’s fellowships and funded internships abroad.
