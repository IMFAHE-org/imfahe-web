International Mentor Program: Accelerate Your Career with Global Experts

Unlock your potential through personalized international mentorship.

Connect with accomplished professionals from world-leading institutions such as Harvard, MIT, Stanford, Google, IBM research, BBVA, Sanofi and many other outstanding institutions through IMFAHE’s International Mentor Program (IMP).

Our global mentoring program connects university students and emerging professionals with international experts who share their knowledge, experience, and professional networks to help the next generation of talent accelerate their careers, develop essential skills, and access global opportunities.

Through personalized online mentoring, participants gain valuable international experience and career guidance—without leaving their home university.

Who Can Participate? 

Mentees: Students from IMFAHE Partner Universities

Students join IMFAHE’s Global Platform of Talent and International Mentoring Program through their university’s annual selection process. Explore your institution’s call for eligibility requirements, application details, and deadlines. Universities usually open the call in October.

Explore Student Open Calls

Global Mentors: Professionals at Leading International Organizations

Share your expertise, inspire emerging talent, and help students shape their futures. If you are interested in joining IMFAHE’s global mentor network, complete our registration form to tell us about your experience and interests. 

Register Your Interest as a Mentor

As an IMFAHE Global Mentor, you can:

  • Inspire and Mentor Future Leaders: Share your expertise through personalized international career mentoring and help talented students and young professionals achieve their goals.
  • Expand Your Global Professional Network: Connect with scientists, entrepreneurs, researchers, and industry leaders from leading universities, research institutions, and companies worldwide.
  • Build International Collaborations: Discover opportunities to collaborate with global experts, develop interdisciplinary projects, and establish strategic partnerships.
  • Launch Startups and Become a Co-Founder: Connect with international entrepreneurial teams, transform innovative ideas into ventures, and explore opportunities to become a startup co-founder.
  • Access Pre-Seed Funding Opportunities: Participate in IMFAHE’s global entrepreneurship initiatives and compete for pre-seed funding to help turn promising ideas into viable startups.
  • Advance Your Own Professional Development: Access specialized training, international masterclasses, and leadership development opportunities tailored to experienced professionals and senior researchers.
  • Shape the Future of Innovation: Contribute your expertise to international entrepreneurship competitions, innovation initiatives, and collaborative projects with global impact.

Your expertise can change someone’s future. Your next big opportunity could start here.
